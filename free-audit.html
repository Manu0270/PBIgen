What is the PBIgen audit?

The PBIgen audit is a free assessment of how a Tableau workbook will migrate to Power BI. It scans the workbook's structural .twb XML and returns an automated conversion score, an estimate of the developer hours remaining and a fixed-cost quote. Three options let you choose how much data to share, from zero data to a live read-only connection.

Audit questions.

What to know before you upload.

What are the three PBIgen audit options?

Option 1 is a code-only audit that needs only the structural .twb file and touches zero data. Option 2 adds an anonymized or mock CSV for a populated Power BI preview. Option 3 uses a read-only database connection to build a fully bound proof-of-concept dashboard.

Can I audit a Tableau workbook without sharing raw data?

Yes. The Option 1 code-only audit parses structural .twb XML only, so zero raw business data is touched or inspected.

What does the audit report include?

An automated conversion score, an estimate of the developer hours remaining, and a fixed-cost quote for the PBIgen team to finish the work. The code-only audit also delivers unpopulated Power BI visual shells (.pbit).

Do I need to provide data for the final turnkey phase?

Yes. The Turnkey Completion Phase, where PBIgen's engineers perform full testing, fine-tuning and measure reconciliation, requires live data connections or representative test data. This ensures numeric accuracy, data model integrity and visual balancing before deployment.
